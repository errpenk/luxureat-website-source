Import into China

Bringing products into the Chinese market

From preliminary assessment to import, customs clearance and distribution, LuxurEat China coordinates the principal stages required to bring products into China.

Work that can be coordinated
  • Preliminary assessment of the product and market-entry model
  • Verification of producer and importer requirements
  • Coordination of GACC / CIFER-related work where applicable
  • Verification of CITA Number-related requirements for applicable certified products
  • HS / CIQ classification checks
  • Chinese labels and import document preparation
  • Coordination of importers, customs and transport
  • Warehousing (including temperature-controlled storage where needed) and distribution coordination
Basic process

Assess

Confirm the product, industry and project objectives.

Verify

Check the relevant documents and applicable requirements.

Prepare

Coordinate registration, labels and import materials.

Import

Coordinate transport, customs clearance and warehousing.

Market

Advance distribution, digital channels and business development according to the project.

Answers & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Popular questions

Did you mean:

Can my product be imported into China?

The specific product needs to be assessed first. Import requirements, timing and permits depend on the product, category, country of origin and selected operating model. LuxurEat China can first conduct a preliminary assessment of the product and market-entry model and verify the relevant documents and requirements.

Can LuxurEat China assist with GACC and CIFER?

LuxurEat China can coordinate work relating to the GACC and CIFER platforms where applicable. Whether they apply, and the specific requirements, need to be verified according to the product.

Can you assist with Chinese labels, HS / CIQ classification and import documents?

Yes. Import support includes HS / CIQ classification, Chinese labels and preparation of relevant documents, with further coordination of importers, customs, transport, warehousing and distribution where required.

Can you assist with a CITA Number?

For applicable certified products, work relating to a CITA Number can be coordinated after the specific product requirements have been verified. Applicability cannot be confirmed in advance without checking the product.

Can you coordinate temperature-controlled storage and distribution?

Depending on the product's storage requirements, LuxurEat China can coordinate suitable warehousing, transport and distribution resources. The need for temperature control and the specific arrangements are confirmed after assessing the product and project scope.

Can you help find Chinese manufacturers?

Yes. Services can include defining product specifications and supplier type, finding manufacturers, obtaining quotations and samples, and conducting preliminary checks of the supplier's Business License, corporate structure, technical materials and existing documents.

How can the risk of working with unverified Chinese suppliers be reduced?

A project can include preliminary verification of supplier corporate information, technical-document review, samples and testing, and coordination of quality control and inspections. Contract matters can be supported through appropriately qualified professionals.

Can you handle export and international logistics after sourcing in China?

Transport, export and customs can be coordinated, together with an assessment of landed costs. However, the purchase price is not the final cost: transport, duties, testing, certification, loss, time and operating risk need to be considered together.

Can LuxurEat China help establish a company in China?

LuxurEat China can coordinate assessment of company form and business activities, company establishment, the Business License, bank accounts, import and export business setup, and local operating matters such as offices, warehouses and personnel.

Does LuxurEat China directly provide legal, tax and contract professional services?

LuxurEat China is responsible for project and operational coordination. Services reserved by law for legal, tax or other professional bodies are provided by appropriately qualified professionals.Contract support is likewise provided through qualified professionals.

Can you help find Chinese distributors and buyers?

Yes. Business-development services include finding potential importers, distributors, buyers and partners, conducting preliminary screening, coordinating bilingual meetings, and following up and assisting with initial negotiations.

Can you assist with market research, trademarks and China's digital channels?

According to the project, LuxurEat China can research relevant market segments and sales channels and coordinate trademark-class assessment, registration and maintenance. China websites, social platforms and marketplaces are assessed according to the brand, product and operating model.

Can you provide trade-fair support in China?

Yes. Services can include selecting suitable trade fairs and events, preparing materials and samples, arranging meeting schedules, and providing a China team, interpreters, on-site assistance and post-event contact follow-up.

What is China Desk?

China Desk is for companies that need to advance business in China continuously. One operating contact coordinates business communications, local partners and needs arising after project launch.

Are services offered at a fixed price?

Contact customer service for details. Pricing is confirmed after assessing the product category, service scope, project complexity and actual work required.
